Standard features

These 16 points apply across the whole Jiuzhou lineup, tricycles and two-wheelers alike.

01
Brand Name

JIUZHOU — a well-known China-based manufacturer, among the first batch of listed motorcycle brands.

02
Headlight

High-intensity LED headlights for improved visibility riding at night.

03
Appearance

Clear digital display of speed and mileage; elegant and intuitive interface.

04
Damping

Double-plated handlebar absorbers (chrome/nickel) with strong spring suspension.

05
Tyre

TNT large-pattern steel-wire tyres — standard 5.00-12, optional 5.50-13.

06
Wheel Rim

Automotive-grade integrated stamping and moulding technology.

07
Frame

Phosphorylation + electrophoresis + baking paint process; double-keel 4-beam chassis for high bearing capacity.

08
Back Bridge

Power-type rear axle, designed for complex road and load conditions.

09
High / Low Speed

Smooth hill-climbing at low speed; stable and fast at high road speed.

10
Leaf Spring

High elasticity plus shock absorption; reduces impact and improves driving comfort.

11
Engine

High power, low fuel consumption, strong acceleration.

12
Carburetor

Kunfu — a reliable, high-efficiency domestic brand.

13
Packing Box

Integrated stamping/moulding; thickened pipes and a durable baking-paint finish.

14
Standard Parts

All parts standardized and approved under national regulations.

15
Fuel Tank

16-litre large-capacity fuel tank.

16
Cushion Backrest

Cold-hair foam with simulated leather for rider comfort.
